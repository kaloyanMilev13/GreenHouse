# Embedded Systems self-study plan

Fontys ICT | First year | Coach: Iman

## A. Rationale - Why Embedded Systems?

I am considering Embedded Systems (Intelligent Technologies) because I enjoy C and understanding how software interacts with hardware. I have started using C, ESP32, Wi-Fi and MQTT in an IoT greenhouse project, and explored alternative communication protocols. This gives me a concrete system in which to investigate sensors, timing and communication decisions. My interest in registers, peripherals, interrupts and digital logic is a direction to investigate, not evidence of mastery. Embedded engineering requires reasoning about physical behaviour, memory, timing and failure as well as writing working code. After discussing this gap with Iman, I want to build the mathematics, electronics and computer science needed to explain why a design works and when another approach is better. This makes the specialisation a considered next step, which I will test through the evidence below.

## B. Learning roadmap - 12 weeks

Scope: 12 weeks at 4.5 hours/week (54 hours). Selected lessons and small experiments, not complete courses. I assume access to my existing ESP32 development setup; no additional sensors, actuators or measuring instruments are assumed. Synthetic data and calculations are acceptable if clearly labelled.

## C. Weekly routine

**Theory - 2 h** - Read/watch only the named extracts; solve or sketch before opening a worked answer.

**Practice - 1.75 h** - Run one controlled experiment: prediction, single change, recorded result.

**Evidence - 0.75 h** - Keep code, parameters, diagram/plot and a 150-word explanation: why, result, limitation, next question.

Total: 4.5 hours/week. An optional fifth hour is for catch-up. If a gate fails, repair the gap in the next practice session and reduce later scope; defer PI if necessary.

## D. Longer-term direction

**Mathematics and electronics:** extend algebra into trigonometry (phase and periodic signals), vectors/matrices (coordinates and coupled systems), then derivatives, integrals and differential equations in depth. Use these to analyse RC dynamics and sensor/actuator response; study logic gates and interface electrical limits.

**Embedded and real-time systems:** deepen architecture, registers, ADC/PWM and UART/I2C/SPI through one peripheral at a time. Then study RTOS scheduling, deadlines, task/ISR communication and race conditions. Demonstrate a measured timing budget and recovery from a failed peripheral.

**Control engineering, then robotics:** progress to modelling, transfer functions, stability analysis, sampling/aliasing, filtering and full PID with actuator constraints. After Linux CLI and C++ or Python basics, explore ROS2 nodes, topics, services and QoS using the [official ROS2 tutorials (source)](https://github.com/ros2/ros2_documentation/blob/jazzy/source/Tutorials.rst). ROS2 supports communication between robotics components; it does not replace low-level control or guarantee deadlines. Start in simulation; choose an OS-compatible supported release at that time.

## E. Progress evaluation

1. **Explain:** at each fortnightly checkpoint, give a five-minute explanation without tutorial notes: principle, implementation, alternative and limitation. Correct gaps before moving on.

2. **Predict and transfer:** make at least one written prediction per phase and solve one changed-input task without copying a solution. Explain any mismatch using units, code or a model.

3. **Reproduce:** keep six evidence bundles with source links, code/version, inputs, assumptions and results. Re-run one experiment with Iman from its recorded steps.

4. **Reason about failure:** by week 12, show evidence for an array-boundary case, a network outage and actuator saturation in simulation; distinguish observations from guarantees.

5. **Decide on fit:** review at weeks 6 and 12. Use two concrete examples of enjoying or disliking hardware/software reasoning, one persistent gap and the workload log to decide: continue, extend foundations or investigate another specialisation.
