 A. Rationale - Why Embedded Systems?
 I am considering Embedded Systems (Intelligent Technologies) because I enjoy C and understanding how
 software interacts with hardware. I have started using C, ESP32, Wi-Fi and MQTT in an IoT greenhouse project,
 and explored alternative communication protocols. This gives me a concrete system in which to investigate
 sensors, timing and communication decisions. My interest in registers, peripherals, interrupts and digital logic is a
 direction to investigate, not evidence of mastery. Embedded engineering requires reasoning about physical
 behaviour, memory, timing and failure as well as writing working code. After discussing this gap with Iman, I want
 to build the mathematics, electronics and computer science needed to explain why a design works and when
 another approach is better. This makes the specialisation a considered next step, which I will test through the
 evidence below.

 C. Weekly routine
 Theory - 2 h - Read/watch only the named extracts; solve or sketch before opening a worked answer.

 Practice - 1.75 h - Run one controlled experiment: prediction, single change, recorded result.

 Evidence - 0.75 h - Keep code, parameters, diagram/plot and a 150-word explanation: why, result, limitation,
 next question.

 Total: 4.5 h/week. A fifth hour is optional catch-up, not new content. If a gate fails, use the next practice session
 to repair the gap and reduce later scope. If necessary, finish week 12 with P control and defer PI; do not
 compress the foundations.

 D. Longer-term direction
 Mathematics and electronics: extend algebra into trigonometry (phase and periodic signals), vectors/matrices
 (coordinates and coupled systems), then derivatives, integrals and differential equations in depth. Use these to
 analyse RC dynamics and sensor/actuator response; study logic gates and interface electrical limits.

 Embedded and real-time systems: deepen architecture, registers, ADC/PWM and UART/I2C/SPI through one
 peripheral at a time. Then study RTOS scheduling, deadlines, task/ISR communication and race conditions.
 Demonstrate a measured timing budget and recovery from a failed peripheral.

 Control engineering, then robotics: progress to modelling, transfer functions, stability analysis,
 sampling/aliasing, filtering and full PID with actuator constraints. After Linux CLI and C++ or Python basics,
 explore ROS2 nodes, topics, services and QoS using the official ROS2 tutorials (source). ROS2 supports
 communication between robotics components; it does not replace low-level control or guarantee deadlines. Start
 in simulation; choose an OS-compatible supported release at that time.

 E. Progress evaluation
 1. Explain: at each fortnightly checkpoint, give a five-minute explanation without tutorial notes: principle,
 implementation, alternative and limitation. Correct gaps before moving on.

 2. Predict and transfer: make at least one written prediction per phase and solve one changed-input task
 without copying a solution. Explain any mismatch using units, code or a model.

 3. Reproduce: keep six evidence bundles with source links, code/version, inputs, assumptions and results.
 Re-run one experiment with Iman from its recorded steps.

 4. Reason about failure: by week 12, show evidence for an array-boundary case, a network outage and
 actuator saturation in simulation; distinguish observations from guarantees.

 5. Decide on fit: review at weeks 6 and 12. Use two concrete examples of enjoying or disliking
 hardware/software reasoning, one persistent gap and the workload log to decide: continue, extend foundations or
 investigate another specialisation.

 Resource access and prerequisites
 Resource titles, linked content and relevance checked on 29 October 2026. The core readings/video pages are publicly accessible;
 no paid course enrolment or certificate is required. Study only the named sections. Entry requirements in the table are my study
 readiness checks, not provider-issued admissions requirements. GNU material includes extensions: focus on portable C and verify
 target-specific details. Espressif API versions vary; select the installed framework/chip version. The ROS2 rendered documentation
 blocked automated access, so its official source was verified instead.
